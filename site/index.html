Rent GPUs from their owners. No company in the middle.

Grid is a compute market on Robinhood Chain. Hosts list their own machines. You pay ETH into a contract and get SSH access straight from the host. You’re charged for every started minute, and the rest comes back to you.

Deploying to the Robinhood Chain testnet now. Where it stands

Three contract calls carry the whole rental.

Billing is per started minute. The money sits in the contract, never with Grid.

  1. Host

    listMachine()

    List a machine

    The GPU, memory, price per hour and the https address of the host’s own node go into the contract.

  2. Renter

    rent()

    Pay for the hours you want

    The ETH for those hours goes into the contract. The host’s node sees the rental on-chain, starts an SSH container and hands you the login once your wallet signature proves the rental is yours.

  3. Either side

    endRental()

    End it any time

    The contract pays the host for every started minute and refunds you the rest. Once the paid time is over, anyone can end it. Both sides collect their ETH with withdraw().

What changes when nobody is in the middle

The same GPU rental, with a contract where the company used to be.

Grid compared with a typical GPU rental platform
A rental platform Grid
Your money Held by the company Held by a contract until the rental ends
Billing Drawn from credit the company holds Per started minute, with the unused rest refunded
Platform fee A cut of every rental None in the contract
Access Issued through the platform Straight from the host’s machine
Who can list Providers the company approves Anyone, with a GRID bond after the first 30 days
Who can switch it off The company Nobody. The market contract has no owner.

Start a host

# in a clone of the repo
$ npm install
$ cp node/env.example node/.env
# set wallet, price, https address
$ npm run node
Your price
Set it per hour, in ETH. A running rental keeps the price it started with.
Paid by the minute
Every started minute is yours. Withdraw your ETH whenever you like.
No cut
The contract takes no fee from what renters pay you.
One container per rental
Each rental runs in its own container on your machine.
No bond for 30 days
Each rental is capped at 0.01 ETH until then. After that you lock GRID to stay listed.
Your switch
Stopping the node pauses your listing.

GRID is the host bond. Nothing else.

Hosts lock it to stay listed. It is never a payment: compute is always paid in ETH, and you don’t need GRID to rent.

Locked to stay listed
After their first 30 days, hosts keep a GRID bond locked to stay in the market.
7 days to come back
When a host unlocks a bond, it comes back to them after 7 days.
Burned only for cheating
Part of a bond is burned only if its host is proven to have cheated a renter.
Launches on Creora
With the Robinhood Chain launch, on the Creora launchpad. The whole supply goes on the bonding curve; there is no team allocation.
